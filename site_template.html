ABOUT SEIF

From the demo room
to the boardroom.

Seif ElBakry founded Seif ElBakry Consultancy after more than two decades inside the region's technology sector — first as an engineer, then leading presales teams — building a practice around one observation: the best technical teams rarely lose deals on capability. They lose them on framing.

Today, Seif works with IT companies and corporate technology teams across Cairo and the GCC, training sales, presales, and technical staff to discover better, pitch tighter, and present solutions clients actually want to sign.

THE METHOD

The value consulting
framework.

Every deal moves through the same four moments. Most technical teams are only ever trained for one of them.

NEED INFORMATION

FAQs about the
consulting practice.

Most sales and presales engagements run three to six months. The first three months focus on training your team and building out your sales and presales cycle, along with the supporting materials. The remaining time is spent monitoring how it's applied and adjusting as it goes.

Every engagement starts before any training does — with a discovery phase into your current pipeline, your team's strengths, and where deals tend to stall. The program that follows is built around your actual sales motion, not a fixed template.

Public courses build individual skills, like pitching and discovery. Company engagements go further — tailored to your product, your sales cycle, and your team, with ongoing coaching built in.

That's most of the practice. Many of the engineers and solution architects Seif trains have never had formal sales training — the program is built to meet them exactly there.
